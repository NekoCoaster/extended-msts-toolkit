# NEMT 1.1.0-alpha.14 — crawl righting and steering

- Hold horn/whistle (Space by default) to gently roll a derailed powered locomotive toward upright along the shorter direction.
- Hold train brake apply (') to steer right or release (;) to steer left around the locomotive's own up axis, including when on its side or upside down.
- Follow MSTS keyboard remapping and modifier bindings; retain normal horn and brake behavior.
- Work at closed throttle and neutral reverser, with pause/focus/strength-zero gates. Keep native collisions and gravity, ease righting near upright, and preserve steering through crawl pitch compensation.

Included with the existing crawl option; no new setting is required. Restart MSTS after updating. The game executable remains unchanged.
