Focused native tracing identifies three additional stored candidates. These have producer evidence, not live field/rate/slip validation. No new loaded vehicle was available for this checkpoint, and no native function was invoked.

| Ordinary wheel rate | car+0x1b0, float32 |0062c679 writes signed longitudinal speed divided by wagon definition+0x448 (WheelRadius) in ordinary branches. A non-powered excess-braking branch writes zero. Radians/second is supported by the formula, not an observed animation-rate comparison. |

The force magnitude at engine+0x2a6 already feeds cab force channels; this checkpoint documents that relation without adding another duplicate quantity. Body/track motion, actual delivered force and wheel visual phase remain separate.
